What you will learn

4 of 9 secure
  • Understand that living things are made up of cells.
  • Understand that microorganisms are usually only a single cell.
  • Describe how scientific knowledge develops over time (cell theory).
  • Use a light microscope and calculate total magnification.
  • Identify the parts of typical plant and animal cells and describe their functions.
  • Compare plant cells and animal cells.
  • Evaluate a model by describing its strengths and limitations.
  • Relate the structures of specialised cells to their functions.
  • Understand how cells are grouped into tissues, organs and organ systems.
